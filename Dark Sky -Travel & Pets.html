📖 How to Play

Movement: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to walk around.

Jumping: Press SPACEBAR to jump across platforms.

Obstacles: Avoid red spikes and moving blocks! Touching them restarts you.

Goal: Reach the glowing pink platform at the end of the course to win coins!
